Explore a little further

A continent like no other.

Antarctica has no sovereign countries or permanent native population. This guide follows five expedition stops around the Antarctic Peninsula and South Shetland Islands.

Ship-based voyages introduce visitors to ice landscapes, wildlife and historic sites. Your operator sets the route and confirms whether each landing can proceed. Start with IAATO’s visitor resources ↗

Explore a little further

Five places to meet the ice.

These are possible landing sites. Wildlife protection, weather and ice conditions determine access.

Neko Harbour

Glaciers frame this mainland landing site in Andvord Bay, where gentoo penguins breed.

Neko Harbour visitor guideline ↗

Cuverville Island

A rocky island in the Errera Channel with a large gentoo penguin colony.

Cuverville Island visitor guideline ↗

Half Moon Island

A South Shetland island with chinstrap penguins and views toward Livingston Island.

Half Moon Island visitor guideline ↗

Goudier Island

The small Port Lockroy island is home to the buildings of historic British Base A.

Goudier Island visitor guideline ↗

Whalers Bay

A bay on Deception Island with remains of a whaling station and the former British base.

Explore a little further

Stories of science & exploration.

Historic buildings and conservation work connect visitors with the human history of the peninsula.

At Port Lockroy on Goudier Island, the UK Antarctic Heritage Trust preserves Base A and welcomes visitors to its museum and post office when conditions allow. Follow guides around historic remains and leave objects where you find them.

Explore a little further

Meals with an expedition crew.

Visitor meals are normally arranged on board by the voyage operator. Ask about menus and dietary requirements before departure.

Antarctica has no traditional national cuisine. Pack and manage any food according to expedition instructions, keep all waste contained and never feed wildlife.

Which currency will I use?

Antarctica has no national currency. The expedition operator sets the currency of its quote and payments; check it along with the cabin, departure and included services.

Before you go

Good to know.

Can I see everything in one day?

Landings depend on the expedition operator, sea ice, weather and site rules. Plan a flexible multi-day voyage with the operator.

Can I book through this website?

This is a student travel guide. Follow the official visitor resources to check current arrangements and contact providers directly.
