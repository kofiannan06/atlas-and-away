About this project.

Atlas & Away is my travel and tourism student project. It brings together 31 countries and five Antarctic expedition stops, helping visitors discover attractions, local culture, food, and activities.

The site combines destination photographs, maps, itineraries, and practical planning tools. Photograph credits and research references are available in each guide, while hotel bookings are completed on the properties’ official websites.
