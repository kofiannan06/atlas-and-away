Welcome to Spain

Get to know the place.

Spain connects Barcelona's Modernist architecture with the Islamic palaces and courtyards of Andalusia. Regional festivals, food markets and Mediterranean beaches add different rhythms to a journey.

Life, traditions & culture.

Valencia's Fallas combine large satirical sculptures, music and fire, while Seville's Feria de Abril celebrates dancing and Andalusian dress. Barcelona's Palau de la Música Catalana reflects Catalan Modernist design.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Regional dishes range from Andalusian gazpacho and Galician octopus to Catalonia's caramel-topped custard.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Browse Barcelona's market stalls, walk beside the Mediterranean and explore the dramatic gorge route of the Caminito del Rey.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Barcelona's Plaça Reial gathers restaurants and evening venues, while Madrid's Teatro Real presents opera and other performances.
