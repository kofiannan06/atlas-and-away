Welcome to Cameroon

Get to know the place.

Cameroon stretches from the Atlantic coast to northern savannahs, with volcanic mountains and rainforest between them. Explore Bamoun royal heritage in Foumban, coastal life in Limbe and the cascades where the Lobé River meets the sea.

Life, traditions & culture.

Ngondo traditions connect the Sawa communities with the Wouri River through ceremonies and public celebrations. Foumban's Bamoun heritage and Kapsiki architecture in Rhumsiki show other strands of the country's cultural life.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Groundnuts, leafy vegetables, cassava and cocoyam feature in dishes whose ingredients and preparation vary across Cameroon's regions.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Forest conservation, volcanic hiking and river journeys offer different ways to encounter Cameroon's landscapes.

When the day slows down.
