Welcome to Costa Rica

Get to know the place.

Costa Rica protects a remarkable range of forests, volcanoes and coastal habitats between two oceans. Cloud-forest trails and wildlife-rich waterways complement the museums and markets of San José.

Life, traditions & culture.

San José's National Theatre reflects the capital's nineteenth-century cultural ambitions, while the National Museum explores Costa Rican history. The Central Market offers a closer look at everyday food and commerce.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Rice, beans, root vegetables and fresh produce underpin home-style dishes commonly served in Costa Rica's small local restaurants, known as sodas.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Explore waterfall paths, forest suspension bridges and the distinctive turquoise river landscape of Tenorio's Río Celeste.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

San José's performance venues include the Teatro Popular Melico Salazar, while Jacó's Pacific waterfront draws visitors for sunset and evening dining.

A few words go a long way.

A starter in Costa Rican Spanish. The pronunciation hints are approximate; listen to local speakers and ask politely when unsure.

Costa Rican Spanish travel phrases
Phrase Meaning Say it approximately
Pura vida All good. Also used as hello or goodbye. POO-rah VEE-dah
Hola Hello OH-lah
Gracias Thank you GRAH-syahs
Disculpe Excuse me dees-KOOL-peh
¿Cuánto cuesta? How much does it cost? KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah
