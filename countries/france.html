Welcome to France

Get to know the place.

France brings together Gothic cathedrals, royal gardens and mountain landscapes. In Paris, the Seine links museum collections, neighborhood cafés and some of the country's best-known monuments.

Life, traditions & culture.

Nice Carnival fills the Riviera city with decorated floats and flower parades. Paris's Palais Garnier and the Festival d'Avignon offer other windows into France's performing arts.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Regional cooking ranges from Burgundy's wine-braised beef to Marseille's fish soup and Lorraine's savory custard tart.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Combine Paris's riverside walks and formal gardens with seasonal skiing in the Chamonix valley.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Paris evenings can include cabaret at the Moulin Rouge, theater performances and views of illuminated monuments along the Seine.
