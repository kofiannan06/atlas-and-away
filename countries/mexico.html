Welcome to Mexico

Get to know the place.

Mexico's living cultures connect ancient cities, modern art and regional cooking. In the capital, monumental plazas and archaeological remains sit within a busy contemporary metropolis.

Life, traditions & culture.

The National Museum of Anthropology introduces Mexico's many Indigenous civilizations and living communities. Day of the Dead observances around Pátzcuaro and Frida Kahlo's art reveal other distinct cultural perspectives.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Mexican regional cooking builds on ingredients such as maize and chiles, with dishes shaped by Indigenous traditions and later cultural exchanges.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Discover Mexico City's canal landscapes and urban parks, or explore the limestone pools around Tulum.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

A walk along central Mexico City's pedestrian streets can lead to evening dining, while Arena México presents the spectacle of lucha libre.

A few words go a long way.

A starter in Mexican Spanish. The pronunciation hints are approximate; listen to local speakers and ask politely when unsure.

Mexican Spanish travel phrases
Phrase Meaning Say it approximately
Buenos días Good morning BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs
¿Habla inglés? Do you speak English? AH-blah een-GLEHS
¿Dónde está el baño? Where is the restroom? DOHN-deh ehs-TAH el BAH-nyoh
¿Cuánto cuesta? How much does it cost? KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah
Gracias Thank you GRAH-syahs
