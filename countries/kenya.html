Welcome to Kenya

Get to know the place.

Kenya rises from the Indian Ocean coast to highlands and Rift Valley landscapes rich in wildlife. Nairobi connects urban museums and conservation projects with national parks, while Mombasa preserves centuries of coastal history.

Life, traditions & culture.

Swahili coastal heritage, Maasai beadwork and the traditions of Kenya's many communities shape its cultural life. Bomas of Kenya and Nairobi's craft markets introduce different forms of performance and making.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Kenyan cooking includes maize staples, beans, leafy greens and grilled meats, with distinct coastal and inland traditions.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Conservation centres and Rift Valley trails add learning and outdoor exploration to a Kenyan journey.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Nairobi offers evening restaurants, music and shopping, with gathering places such as The Village Market adding to the city's social life.
