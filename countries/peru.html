Welcome to Peru

Get to know the place.

Peru links Pacific coast, Andean mountains and Amazon rainforest with the remains of many ancient societies. Lima's food traditions and the Inca landscapes around Cusco make contrasting starting points for exploring the country.

Life, traditions & culture.

Cusco's Inti Raymi celebrates the Inca sun tradition, while Puno's Virgen de la Candelaria festival brings together music, dance and religious devotion. Quechua and Aymara communities sustain important strands of living Andean culture.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Peruvian cooking draws on coastal seafood, Andean crops and Indigenous, African, European and Asian culinary traditions.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Explore Lima's Pacific-facing neighbourhoods or follow the Inca routes that connect archaeological sites in the Andes.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Barranco's streets and arts venues offer an evening atmosphere distinct from Lima's illuminated fountain displays at the Circuito Mágico del Agua.
