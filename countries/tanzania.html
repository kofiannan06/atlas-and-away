Welcome to Tanzania

Get to know the place.

Tanzania links the plains of the Serengeti and the volcanic highlands with the Swahili coast and Zanzibar. Wildlife landscapes, trading towns and community-led cultural visits reveal different sides of this East African country.

Life, traditions & culture.

Swahili culture shapes Zanzibar's architecture, language and music, including taarab. Sauti za Busara brings African musical traditions to Stone Town, while mainland communities maintain many different cultural practices.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Tanzanian meals range from grilled skewers and maize staples to coastal rice dishes, coconut flavours and small sweet cakes.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Combine waterfall walks and agricultural village visits with a different view of the Serengeti's open plains.

When the day slows down.

Stone Town's waterfront and Forodhani Gardens are associated with evening gatherings and food stalls beside the sea.
