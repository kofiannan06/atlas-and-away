Welcome to India

Get to know the place.

India's regions offer a vast range of landscapes, languages, faiths and food traditions. In Delhi, Mughal monuments and crowded bazaars sit alongside the avenues and memorials of the modern capital.

Life, traditions & culture.

Durga Puja in Kolkata combines religious celebration with temporary artistic installations, while Rajasthan's Pushkar Fair has its own local traditions. Delhi's Dilli Haat introduces crafts and food from across the country.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Indian cooking is deeply regional, with Delhi's rich gravies, southern fermented-batter dishes and Hyderabad's aromatic rice offering only a small introduction.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Browse Old Delhi's bazaars, travel Kerala's backwaters and experience a historic Himalayan railway.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Mumbai's Marine Drive and Kolkata's Park Street offer two different settings for an evening walk, food and city views.
