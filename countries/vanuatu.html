Welcome to Vanuatu

Get to know the place.

Vanuatu's islands combine coral-fringed coastlines, freshwater blue holes and volcanic landscapes. Ni-Vanuatu kastom, including oral traditions, village customs and distinctive arts, remains an important part of life across the archipelago.

Life, traditions & culture.

Kastom carries ancestral knowledge through ceremonies, stories, art and community practice, with different traditions across Vanuatu's islands. Pentecost's nagol land diving and Vanuatu sand drawing are two distinctive examples.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Root crops, coconut cream, banana leaves and hot-stone cooking are central to ni-Vanuatu food, alongside seafood and dishes shaped by outside influences.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Snorkelling excursions and Santo's spring-fed blue holes offer ways to explore Vanuatu's marine and freshwater environments.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Port Vila's waterfront offers evening dining and sunset views, while nakamals have a distinct place in the social tradition of sharing kava.
