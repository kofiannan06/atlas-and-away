Welcome to Colombia

Get to know the place.

Colombia's Caribbean shores, Andean cities and tropical forests create striking changes of landscape and climate. Cartagena's fortifications and Bogotá's museums introduce a country shaped by Indigenous, African and European histories.

Life, traditions & culture.

Cartagena's palenquera traditions connect the city with Afro-Colombian heritage, while Bogotá's Gold Museum presents the artistry of pre-Hispanic societies. Fernando Botero's work adds a distinct modern artistic voice.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Colombian food changes markedly by region, from Caribbean fried arepas to Bogotá's potato soups and the generous platters of the paisa region.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Follow Cartagena's walls, ride above Medellín's hills or climb Guatapé's rock for different views of Colombian settlement and landscape.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Cartagena's historic squares provide settings for evening dining and music, while Caribbean rhythms are an important part of the city's cultural life.

Hotel within Cartagena's walled city, incorporating a former convent dating to 1621, with colonial courtyards, an outdoor pool, restaurants, bars and spa facilities.
