Welcome to Jamaica

Get to know the place.

Jamaica combines Caribbean beaches and terraced waterfalls with a powerful musical heritage. Kingston's museums and historic homes reveal the people and creative traditions behind the island's global influence.

Life, traditions & culture.

Reggae and dancehall are central to Jamaica's creative identity, with Kingston recognized as a UNESCO Creative City of Music. The National Gallery and Institute of Jamaica broaden the story through art and heritage collections.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Jamaican cooking layers African, European and Asian influences through dishes such as jerk chicken, ackee and saltfish, patties and rice and peas.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Balance Montego Bay's beach time with Kingston's urban green spaces and the garden setting around YS Falls.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Kingston's reggae and dancehall scene brings music into the evening, while Negril's west-facing coast provides a setting for sunset views.
