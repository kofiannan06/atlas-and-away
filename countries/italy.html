Welcome to Italy

Get to know the place.

Italy's cities place ancient streets beside Renaissance paintings and lively public squares. Beyond Rome and Florence, Venice's canals and Liguria's coastal paths reveal very different landscapes.

Life, traditions & culture.

Venice's masked Carnival draws on a long tradition of costume and public spectacle. Opera at Milan's La Scala and Siena's neighborhood loyalties around the Palio reflect other local traditions.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Italian cooking changes by region, from Naples's soft-centered pizza to Milan's saffron risotto and Sicily's fried rice snacks.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Discover canal views in Venice, Roman roadside monuments and walking routes between the Cinque Terre villages.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Rome's Piazza Navona and Venice's Rialto waterfront offer evening walks among illuminated buildings, cafés and restaurants.

Rome

Hotel Artemide

Located on Via Nazionale in central Rome, this hotel offers rooms and suites, the Ambrosia rooftop restaurant, and a spa near the Repubblica and Termini areas.
