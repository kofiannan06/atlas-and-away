Welcome to Brazil

Get to know the place.

Atlantic beaches, Amazon forest and powerful waterfalls give Brazil an extraordinary range of landscapes. In Rio and Salvador, samba, capoeira and distinctive regional cooking bring the country's cultural history into everyday life.

Life, traditions & culture.

Rio's samba schools and Bahia's capoeira traditions express distinct strands of Afro-Brazilian culture. Salvador's Pelourinho also connects architecture, music and the heritage of its Black communities.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Cassava, beans, seafood and regional ingredients such as dendê palm oil connect Brazil's Indigenous, African and European culinary influences.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Rio's mountain transport and botanical garden offer several ways to see how the city meets its forested hills.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Rio's evenings include Lapa's samba venues, while the Sambadrome becomes a major stage for samba-school parades during Carnival.
