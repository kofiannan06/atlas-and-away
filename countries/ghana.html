Welcome to Ghana

Get to know the place.

Ghana's coast holds fishing towns, historic forts and the capital, Accra, while forest landscapes lie inland. Markets, festivals and food connect visitors with a country whose heritage includes both independence and the painful history of the Atlantic slave trade.

Life, traditions & culture.

The Ga Homowo festival remembers the overcoming of famine, while Asante Akwasidae ceremonies honour ancestors and royal heritage. In Accra, Makola Market reveals the daily work of traders and makers.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Fermented maize, cassava, rice, beans and plantain underpin many Ghanaian meals, with sauces and accompaniments varying by region.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Explore rainforest canopy bridges, historic botanical collections and community-protected forest wildlife.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Accra's Osu area and Labadi waterfront bring together evening food, music and social life.
