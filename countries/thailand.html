Welcome to Thailand

Get to know the place.

Thailand's river cities and former capitals preserve royal buildings, Buddhist temples and distinctive regional traditions. From Bangkok's Chao Phraya waterfront to the northern mountains, markets and cooking add everyday texture to the journey.

Life, traditions & culture.

Songkran marks the Thai New Year with water-related customs, family gatherings and respect for elders. Bangkok's silk heritage and Muay Thai traditions offer other ways to encounter Thai cultural life.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Thai regional cooking brings together rice, noodles, fresh herbs and combinations of sour, sweet, salty and spicy flavours.

Make a day of it.

River transport, large markets and northern forest landscapes offer different ways to explore Thailand.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Bangkok's Khaosan Road is associated with a busy evening restaurant and bar scene, while Chiang Mai's Night Bazaar centres on shopping and food.
