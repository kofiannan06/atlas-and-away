Welcome to Fiji

Get to know the place.

Fiji's islands combine coral reefs and tropical coastlines with forested interiors and busy market towns. Indigenous iTaukei and Indo-Fijian traditions help shape the food, faiths and everyday life of this Pacific nation.

Life, traditions & culture.

Meke combines song, dance and storytelling in iTaukei tradition, while Navala's bure architecture and Suva's markets reveal other aspects of local life. Indo-Fijian communities contribute important religious and culinary traditions.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Fijian food brings together root crops, coconut, seafood and earth-oven cooking, alongside the spices and dishes of Indo-Fijian kitchens.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Reef excursions, surf viewing and Sabeto's geothermal pools offer varied ways to experience Fiji beyond its towns.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Suva's restaurants and bars offer a city evening, while harbour views and cultural performances provide quieter ways to spend time after sunset.
