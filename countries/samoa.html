Welcome to Samoa

Get to know the place.

Samoa's volcanic islands hold lava fields, waterfalls and coastal villages surrounded by tropical vegetation. Fa'a Samoa, the Samoan way of life, gives family, community and cultural practice a central place in the country's identity.

Life, traditions & culture.

Fa'a Samoa places strong emphasis on family, community and respect. Siva dance, siva afi fireknife performance, siapo barkcloth and the ceremonial sharing of 'ava are distinct expressions of Samoan culture.

Explore a little further

A taste of the place.

Samoan meals often combine taro or breadfruit with coconut, fish, pork or chicken, with umu cooking important for shared feasts.

Explore a little further

Make a day of it.

Forest trails, volcanic coastal scenery and Savai'i's waterfalls offer ways to explore Samoa's island landscapes.

Explore a little further

When the day slows down.

Fiafia evenings bring together food, music and dance, often including siva afi; current programmes vary between venues.
